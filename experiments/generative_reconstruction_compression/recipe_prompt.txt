
Write a detailed, practical sourdough bread recipe. The recipe should be written for a home baker with intermediate experience.

Requirements:
1. Begin with a short introduction (2-3 sentences) explaining what makes this sourdough distinctive.
2. Include a clearly labelled Ingredients section with exact quantities in both metric and imperial.
3. Include a Timeline section that breaks the process into Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 stages.
4. The method should be detailed: explain the purpose of each step (e.g., why autolyse improves gluten development), not just what to do.
5. Include a Troubleshooting section with at least 4 common problems and solutions.
6. End with serving suggestions and storage instructions.
7. Do not use any markdown formatting such as bold, italics, or headers with # symbols. Use plain text with all-caps for section headings (e.g., INGREDIENTS, METHOD).
8. Be specific: use precise temperatures (degrees Celsius and Fahrenheit), exact times, and visual cues to tell bakers when each stage is complete.
