
Write a short story of approximately 600-800 words. The story should be in the mystery genre.

Setting: A remote lighthouse on a rocky coast during a stormy autumn night.
Protagonist: An aging lighthouse keeper named Edmund who has tended the light for thirty years.
Plot: A small rowing boat washes ashore carrying a waterlogged leather satchel. Inside the satchel, Edmund finds a series of handwritten letters addressed to someone named "V." and a compass engraved with a name he recognizes. Over the course of the night, as the storm worsens, Edmund pieces together what happened to the ship that sank offshore twenty years ago — an event that changed his life.

Requirements:
1. Write in third-person limited perspective.
2. Use vivid, sensory descriptions: the smell of salt and oil, the sound of the wind and the Fresnel lens rotating, the cold, the dark.
3. Include at least one flashback.
4. End with an ambiguous, unresolved conclusion — do NOT explain everything.
5. Do not use any markdown formatting. Write only the story text itself, starting with the story's first sentence.
